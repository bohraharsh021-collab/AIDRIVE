SUBJECT: DIGITAL PRINCIPLES & SYSTEM DESIGN (ECE 1ST YEAR)

PROBLEM:

F(A, B, C) = (A AND B) OR (NOT A AND C) OR (B AND C)

TASKS FOR THE ELVES:
1. Construct the complete 8-row Truth Table.
2. Identify all minterms (Sigma m) and maxterms (Pi M).
3. Apply Boolean algebra theorems / De Morgan's laws or K-Map simplification.
4. Draw or specify the minimal 2-level NAND-NAND implementation.
