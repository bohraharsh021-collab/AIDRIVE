EXPERIMENT: SERIES RLC RESONANCE FREQUENCY ANALYSIS
DEPARTMENT: ELECTRONICS AND COMMUNICATION ENGINEERING (BIT SATHY)

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Given a series RLC circuit with the following component values:
- Resistance R = 20 Ohms
- Inductance L = 5 mH (0.005 H)
- Capacitance C = 2.2 uF (0.0000022 F)

TASKS FOR THE ELVES:
1. Calculate the resonant frequency f0 (in Hz) and angular resonant frequency omega0 (in rad/s).
2. Calculate the Quality Factor Q and Bandwidth BW.
3. Write a standard ANSI C program to compute impedance across frequencies from 100 Hz to 10 kHz in steps of 500 Hz.
4. Prepare a clean lab record summary with formula derivations and tabulation format.
